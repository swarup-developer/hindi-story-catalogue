### How each platform charges

Prices are what each platform listed in October 2026. They change often, so check the app before you pay.

| Platform | Free part | Paid part |
|---|---|---|
| **Pratilipi** | Most stories are free to read on the app and website. | Some long series lock their newest parts — unlock them with coins or wait for them to open. Titles by "Pratilipi Premium" need a Premium subscription. |
| **Pocket FM** | The first episodes of every show, plus about 30 free minutes per show each day. Very short shows are completely free. | After that, episodes cost coins (about 0.9 coins per minute) or you can get Premium (₹129/month in India) for extra daily free time. |
| **Audible** | Podcasts and audio shows (Bhay Originals, Bhoot Kaal, Vikram Beetaal and more) are free after signing in. | Audiobooks and some Audible Originals need a membership (₹199/month after a 30-day free trial) or a one-time purchase. |
| **Kuku FM** | A few sample episodes, sometimes posted on YouTube. | Subscription for the full catalogue. |
| **Netflix / Prime Video / JioHotstar / SonyLIV** | Trailers only. | Monthly subscription (Prime Video comes with Amazon Prime). Older films are sometimes only available to rent. |
| **YouTube, Spotify, Amazon Music** | Everything listed here is free (with ads). | — |
| **Kindle and paperbacks** | Free samples on Amazon. | Buy the book, or read free if it is part of Kindle Unlimited. |
