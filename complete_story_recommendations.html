Why this catalogue exists

Some stories disappear from platform databases, move to a new collection, become region-limited, or stop appearing in normal search results. This catalogue is kept as a practical discovery index so users can still find the original source, author/platform page, or a verified discovery page when the platform’s own search is difficult to use.

Verification policy: links are added only when a legitimate platform, publisher, author, IMDb, or official discovery page was found. A direct title page is preferred. If a stable individual page was not available, the entry is labelled as a discovery/search link rather than being presented as a direct story page. Episode counts and runtimes are shown only when a public catalogue record supports them; otherwise the card says “Not publicly listed.” Always confirm the title and rights on the destination platform before reading, downloading, sharing, or purchasing.

This catalogue is designed for GitHub Pages and uses no external JavaScript, images, or tracking libraries. It can be hosted as a standalone HTML file.
